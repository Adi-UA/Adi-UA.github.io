A Java Fabric mod published on [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/fertile-grounds) and [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fertile-grounds). Three craftable soil tiers give each random tick a 15% or 50% chance to bone-meal the crop on top, about 4x to 11x faster growth, while still behaving like vanilla soil. Version 1.2.0 added the Bean Fairy, which can visit at night and fully grow a 5x5 patch of crops. Each supported Minecraft version lives on its own branch.
